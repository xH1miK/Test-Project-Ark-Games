# Промпты исходников

Сгенерировано встроенным `image_gen`. Для девяти UI-ассетов использовался прозрачный фон PNG и общий стиль:

> casual mobile game UI asset, bold readable shapes, soft gradients, glossy highlight, subtle dark outline, centered, transparent background, no text, no watermark

| Файл | Основное описание |
|---|---|
| `coin.png` | Front-facing gold coin with a thick raised rim and one unmistakable embossed miner hard-hat emblem; warm yellow-orange gold; simple silhouette readable at 60 px. |
| `coin_plate.png` | Wide horizontal pill-shaped UI panel; dark navy-brown fill, thin golden inner border, empty center, about 3:1. |
| `pad_upgrade.png` | Horizontal rounded purple floor badge with pale lavender bevel; compact solid white bulldozer pictogram entirely within the left third; remaining two thirds blank for a price label; top-down. |
| `pad_unlock.png` | Long rounded purple floor banner with pale lavender bevel; bold white padlock on the left; rest blank for a price label; top-down. |
| `joystick_base.png` | Perfectly circular, semi-transparent white joystick ring with a soft inner glow and a transparent center; flat top-down view. |
| `joystick_knob.png` | Perfectly round, solid white glossy joystick disc with a soft drop shadow. |
| `sound_on.png` | Compact white speaker with exactly two bold curved sound waves, chunky rounded geometry, thin dark outline; legible at 60 px. |
| `sound_off.png` | Compact white speaker with a bold red diagonal slash, no sound waves, chunky rounded geometry, thin dark outline; legible at 60 px. |
| `win_ribbon.png` | Wide golden celebratory ribbon with folded ends and an empty center for a title. |

`ground.png` использует отдельный промпт без общего стиля и без прозрачности:

> Seamless tileable texture of a dark purple-gray cave stone floor, stylized hand-painted game texture, large soft flat stones with subtle cracks and small pebbles, low contrast, even top-down lighting, no shadows, no perspective, square 1024x1024. Opposite edges must match for a repeating tile; no border or vignette.

Исходники сохранены в разрешении, которое выдал генератор; ресайз и WebP не выполнялись.
